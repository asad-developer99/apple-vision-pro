Photos and Videos

Be in the moment.
All over again.

Your memories

Vision Pro is Apple’s first 3D camera. You can capture magical spatial photos and spatial videos in 3D, then relive those cherished moments like never before with immersive Spatial Audio. Your existing library of photos and videos looks incredible at remarkable scale. And panoramas wrap around you — making you feel like you’re standing right where you took them.

Technology

Innovation you can
see, hear, and feel.

Pushing boundaries from the inside out.
Spatial experiences on Vision Pro are only possible through groundbreaking Apple technology. Displays the size of a postage stamp that deliver more pixels than a 4K TV to each eye. Incredible advances in Spatial Audio. A revolutionary dual‑chip design featuring custom Apple silicon.

More pixels than a 4K TV. For each eye.

The custom micro‑OLED display system features 23 million pixels, delivering stunning resolution and colors.

Our most advanced
Spatial Audio system ever.

Dual-driver audio pods positioned next to each ear deliver personalized sound while letting you hear what’s around you.

Responsive, precision eye tracking.

A high-performance eye tracking system of LEDs and infrared cameras projects invisible light patterns onto each eye.

A sophisticated sensor array.

A pair of high-resolution cameras transmit over one billion pixels per second to the displays.

Revolutionary dual‑chip
performance.

The powerful M2 chip simultaneously runs visionOS and graphics, while the brand-new R1 chip streams images to the displays within 12 milliseconds.
